# Notes template (one file per video)

```markdown
# [NNN] <video title>
- Source: <URL or file> · Author/channel: <…> · Duration: <…> · Date: <if known>
- Evidence: transcript (captions | whisper) / frames (N) / Gemini · Language: <…>
- Reliability: <expert with figures | opinion | ad for a product…>

## Main thesis
One or two sentences.

## Key ideas
- <idea, stated operationally> [NNN @mm:ss]

## Frameworks and methods
### <name of the method>
1. step… [NNN @mm:ss]
2. …

## Figures, benchmarks, thresholds
- <figure + context + condition> [NNN @mm:ss]

## Examples and case studies
- <case: context → action → result> [NNN @mm:ss]

## Tools and resources
- <tool: what for>

## Mistakes to avoid
- …

## Shown on screen (frames only)
- <slide/diagram/dashboard: content> [@mm:ss]

## Ready-to-use content
Scripts, hooks, templates, formulas quoted or closely paraphrased.

## Open questions / doubts
Questionable claims, outdated info, contradictions with other videos.
```

Aim for 60–200 lines depending on how dense the video is. A 10-minute video with nothing
actionable can stay short; say so.
